Meet MyFestivo

Our first passionate product, built for college events.
Visit MyFestivo ↗

The problem

Colleges and Universities across India run events through a scattered mix of Google forms, WhatsApp groups, Excel sheets and manual UPI checks. Organisers get stressed and students struggle to find and participate in events.

Our solution

We are building an Event Operating System - One platform for everything, from registration to hosting and managing events.

List events, manage registrations, collect payments, generate tickets, assign roles and tasks, chat with participants and perform event day check-ins.

Discover and follow events, invite teammates seamlessly, and register securely with RSVP in just a few clicks.
